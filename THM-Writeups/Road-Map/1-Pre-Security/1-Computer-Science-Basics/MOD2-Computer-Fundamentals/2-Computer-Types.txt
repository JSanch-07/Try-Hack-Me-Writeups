Intro Summary:
	. Computers come in many forms, from standard laptop and desktop computers to smart fridges and dishwashers.

Goal of this room:
	. Identify and distinguish between different types of computer systems.

Sophia's Summer of Hidden Computers - Month 1:
	. Not all computers are meant:
		. To move
		. For people to sit in front of
	. Different Types of Computer Systems (1):
		. Laptop:
			. Use a screen and keyboard and are used for portable everyday computing
			. Caveat: Staying cool is difficult due to the small form-factor and use of a battery
		. Desktop:
			. Use a screen and keyboard and are used for sustained performance at a fixed location
			. Caveat: Stationary and gets its power from a wall outlet
		. Workstation:
			. Use a screen and keyboard and are used for precision and reliability for professional tasks
			. Caveat: Stationary and wall powered but also uses complex components to ensure accuracy when performing complex calculations/tasks
		. Server:
			. Do not use a screen and keyboard and are used for providing services to many users over a network

Sophia's Summer of Hidden Computers - Month 2:
	. Different Types of Computer Systems (2):
		. Smartphone: Pocket-sized computer optimized for battery life and internet connectivity
			. Example: iPhone, Android phone, Samsung phone
		. Tablet: Touch-first computer with a larger screen than a smartphone
			. Examples: iPad, drawing tablet, Kindle
		. IoT Device: Network-connected device with a single purpose
			. Examples: Thermostat, smart doorbell, fitness tracker (watch)
		. Embedded Computer: Computer built into another device
			. Example: Coffee maker controller, automatic door sensor, lamp dimmer chip
	. IoT vs Embedded:
		. Both IoT and Embedded computers can be small and single-purpose
		. The difference is connectivity:
			. IoT devices connect to a network to report data/receive commands
			. Embedded computers might not connect to anything at all (they do their job inside of the machine)
				. Embedded: invisible, reliable, and everywhere

Why Computers Come in Different Flavors:
	. Every computer design has a trade-off
		. Smaller, more compact computers sacrifice performance
		. Larger computers sacrifice mobility
	. Purpose shapes everything when debating which form factor a specific computer should be
	. Lab Notes:
		. Why are laptops more prone to overheating?
			. Laptops use tiny fans, heat pipes, and a heat sink
			. Although these methods work for the most part, they cannot keep up with the standard cooling methods for desktop computers
		. Since servers support a business 24/7, how do you protect the servers from a power outage?
			. Redundant power reduces a single failure point
				. Example: A server has 2 power source cables rather than 1
		. Computer types and applications examples:
			. Edit a 4K video for hours on end: Workstation
			. Host a website 24/7: Server
			. Ring when button is pressed: Embedded computer
		. Practice Questions:
			1. Why do laptops throttle more than desktops?
				. Answer: Less cooling space
			2. What does server redundancy prevent?
				. Answer: Prevents a single point of failure
			3. Why do smartphones last longer on battery than laptops?
				. Optimized for power efficiency
			4. ECC RAM and certified drivers are common features for which type of computer?
				. Answer: Workstation
			5. In many smart homes, what coordinates devices?
				. Answer: Hub/Cloud service
