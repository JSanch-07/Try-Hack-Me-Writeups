Introduction:
	. Reasons for cloud computing:
		. Multiple users attempting to connect to an app at once
		. You want your app to be accessible without having to keep your own server running 24/7
		. Reduce lag for foreign users

Cloud Computing Overview:
	. Cloud Computing Use Cases:
		. Move locally stored files to online storage (save physical space)
		. Run your application using cloud provided resources rather than your own
		. Cloud hosted applications are easy to access, reliable, and scalable (ready to grow with the increasing number of users)
	. The Evolution of the Cloud:
		. Physical Servers (1960s-2000)
			. Physical servers in company buildings
			. One server = One Job
			. Expensive and slow to scale
		. Virtualization (1999-2006)
			. Multiple virtual servers on one machine
			. Better hardware utilization
			. Faster provisioning
		. Automation & Remote Management (2003-2006)
			. Servers managed over the internet
			. Early automation infrastructure
		. Cloud Computing - AWS Launch (2006)
			. Rent virtual computers & storage on demand
			. No hardware ownership
			. Elastic scaling in minutes
		. Modern Cloud Era (2012-today)
			. AWS, Azure, Google Cloud
			. Containers
			. Focus on apps, not servers
			. Global-scale platforms
	. Cloud Benefits and Characteristics
		. The cloud was designed to address the problems of limited capacity, high costs, and slow growth
			. Scalability - Easily scale up or down depending on your applications usage rates
			. On-demand self-service - Create or remove servers and storage instantly (without waiting for hardware)
			. Pay only for what you use - You are charged based on usage, not upfront costs
			. Security - Cloud providers protect the infrastructure with strong security measures
			. High availability - Applications keep running even if the system fails
			. Global Access - Your app can be accessed worldwide
	. Types of Cloud
		. The flexibility provided by the cloud allows apps to be run in different ways
			. Deployment types:
				. Public cloud - Used by startups, websites, and global apps because it's affordable, scalable, and requires no infrastructure management
				. Private cloud - Used by banks, healthcare, and government organizations because it offers greater control and compliance for sensitive data when compared to public clouds
				. Hybrid cloud - Used by companies like e-commerce platforms that need to keep sensitive data private while still scaling publicly during high demand
			. Different Ways to Use Cloud Services:
				. Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS) - Renting an empty apartment:
					. You rent basic computing resources (virtual servers, storage, networking)
					. You are responsible for managing the OS and your app while the provider manages physical hardware
				. Platform as a Service (PaaS) - Renting a semi-furnished apartment:
					. The cloud provider manages the infrastructure and the OS
					. You focus on building, running, and deploying your app without worrying about servers
				. Software as a Service (SaaS) - Staying at a hotel:
					. You use a complete application over the internet
					. The provider manages everything while you just access the software through a browser/app
					. Examples: Gmail, Zoom, Netflix
	. Major Cloud Vendors
		. Microsoft Azure - A strong competitor (common for enterprise / hybrid cloud environments)
		. Google Cloud Platform (GCP) - Known for powerful data analytics, AI, and machine learning tools
		. Alibaba Cloud - A major player in Asia, offering competitive global cloud services
		. IBM Cloud - Focuses on hybrid cloud and AI-driven solutions for business
		. Oracle Cloud - Focuses on enterprise applications and databases
	. How Companies Are Using the Cloud
		. Netflix - runs its entire platform on AWS so it can stay online and scale globally
		. Spotify - uses the cloud to handle millions of songs and users
		. Instagram - relies on the cloud to store massive amounts of photos and videos
		. Online Stores - use the cloud to handle traffic spikes during events like Black Friday without buying permanent physical infrastructure
	. Summary Questions:
		1. What is the characteristic of cloud environments that enables you to handle an unexpected increase in access to your application?
			. Answer: Scalability
		2. What is the most common type of cloud deployment used?
			. Answer: Public Cloud
		3. Suppose you want to deploy an application to the internet, focusing only on application development and leaving infrastructure to others. What type of cloud service is the best?
			. Answer: Platform as a Service (PaaS)

Deploying a Cloud Instance:
	. EC2 (Virtual Computer/Server):
		. EC2 represents a virtual computer in the cloud
		. Whenever you add an EC2 instance, you are adding a computer to your environment
	. Instance Type:
		. Instance types describe how powerful the virtual computer is
			. Depends on hardware factors like CPU and RAM
			. More powerful = Higher cost
